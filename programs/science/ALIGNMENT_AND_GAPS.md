# Pure science as language direction — limits

CubalC models **laws as pure logic** (integers, cubes, plugs, flow). That is a **language design direction**.

## What the language can express well
- Discrete arithmetic and integer science relations
- Systems: entity (cube), relation (plug), process (flow), containment (nest)
- Scaled public-domain constants

## Out of scope here
- Host physics engine or wet lab
- Floating-point CAS

Gaps drive **language enhancement** (more pure ops, better scales).
