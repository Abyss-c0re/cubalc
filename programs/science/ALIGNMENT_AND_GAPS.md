# Pure science as language direction — limits

CubalC models **laws as pure logic** (integers, cubes, plugs, flow). That is a **language design direction**, not an educational institution.

## What the language can express well
- Discrete arithmetic and integer science relations
- Systems: entity (cube), relation (plug), process (flow), containment (nest)
- Scaled public-domain constants

## What it is not
- Not a school / diploma program
- Not a host physics engine or wet lab
- Not floating-point CAS

Gaps drive **language enhancement** (more pure ops, better scales), not diploma paperwork.
